How I sell

Don't pitch.
Ask.

Every call follows one structure, based on Question-Based Selling. The prospect builds their own case for buying, so the decision sticks after the call ends.

01

Frame the call

Set the agenda, ask permission to ask questions, and make it clear I'll say so if it's not the right fit.

02

Discovery

Why now? What triggered the call? Who's involved? Listen to understand, not to reply — and write down their exact words.

03

Build the gap

Where they are today, where they want to be, and what staying put costs them. In their words, in their numbers.

The sale is made here
04

Transition

Only when they've named the consequence and described the result they want. Never because the clock says so.

05

Present & commit

Short and specific: three pillars at most, each tied to something they said. Then a read on how they're feeling.

06

Close & follow up

A real next step, booked on the call, with two set time slots. Objections handled honestly — never with fake urgency.

"If we're not the right fit, I'll tell you. Either way, the decision is yours."

No invented urgency

No fake deadlines, made-up discounts or "only two spots left". Buyers can tell, and it hurts your brand.

No promises you can't keep

I only use claims, results and examples that are true. Every client is a long-term reference for your business.

Detached from the deal

I care about the prospect getting the right result, not about one more close. People can hear the difference.

Questions

How do you charge?

Usually a retainer plus commission on closed deals, or commission only for offers with a strong, proven lead flow. We agree on the model on our intro call, once I understand your offer, price point and call volume.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your booking calendar and CRM, your offer details and pricing, any call recordings or FAQs you already have, and an hour to walk me through your ideal client. I'll take it from there.

Do you use high-pressure tactics?

No. No fake scarcity, no invented discounts, no guilt. I close by helping people see their own problem clearly. Clients who buy that way stay, refer and don't ask for refunds.

Which time zones do you cover?

I work remotely from Europe, so UK and EU hours are home ground, with overlap for US East Coast and Brazil calls.

Can you also fix my pipeline?

Yes. I build CRM and workflow systems in monday.com and HubSpot as part of my day job, so if leads are slipping between booking and close, I can set that up too.
